EuroConsole guide

Every Eurorack module is a bank of 4 channels: 3U, 24HP (6HP per channel), one QTP in and one QTP out (SUN · SKY · GRASS · EARTH). Channel banks sit left and right, the master section in the middle. Click a channel module to remove it from its bank, an empty slot to add it, the preamp to switch Mic / Line / Direct; pick a master module to change or remove it, or + in a master's heading to add one. No summing module sums more than 8 channels: each bus is summed in groups of 8 (1–8, 9–16 …), then the groups are summed (Σ).
