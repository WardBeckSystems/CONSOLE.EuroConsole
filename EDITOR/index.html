EuroConsole guide

Every Eurorack module is a bank of 4 channels: 3U, 24HP (6HP per channel), one QTP in and one QTP out (SUN · SKY · GRASS · EARTH). Channel banks sit left and right, the master section in the middle. Click a channel module to remove it from its bank, an empty slot to add it, the preamp to switch Mic / Line / Direct; pick a master module to change or remove it, or + in a master's heading to add one. No summing point takes more than 8 inputs: each bus is summed per 8 channels in a group sum that sits under those channels' banks, then the master sums the groups (Σ); every sum face shows its inputs per summing point. Meters are vertical, one per bus, like the M467's.
