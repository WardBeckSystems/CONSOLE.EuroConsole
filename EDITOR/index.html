Channels

Every Eurorack module is a bank of 4 channels (one QTP in, one QTP out): 4 preamps, 4 EQs, 4 compressors, 4 faders. Each column is a bank, top to bottom. Click the preamp for Mic, Line or Direct; click a module to add or remove it for the bank; click a row name to set it on every bank. Inserts and fader sit in the M480B fader send / return; the sends are post-fader, each on one QTP.
