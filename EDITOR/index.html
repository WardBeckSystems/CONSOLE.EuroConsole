EuroConsole guide

Every Eurorack module is a bank of 4 channels: 3U, 24HP (6HP per channel), one QTP in and one QTP out (SUN · SKY · GRASS · EARTH). Channel banks sit left and right, the master section in the middle. Click a channel module to remove it from its bank, an empty slot to add it, the preamp to switch Mic / Line / Direct; pick a master module to change or remove it, or + in a master's heading to add one. Every send bank takes the bank's PRE (insert return) and POST (fader out), sets every send pre or post, sums its 4 channels per bus inside the module with the bus coming in from the previous bank, and passes the bus on to the next bank or the master: no summing point takes more than 5 inputs. Everything is digitally controlled analog. Meters are vertical, one per channel or bus. Power figures are first estimates.
